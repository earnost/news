Business

The Cost of Compute

In recent times, companies have supposed that the reduction of employees coupled with increased use of AI would yield a net positive for the company. After a series of "AI driven layoffs", some companies have realized that this is not the case.
Ford Motor Company recently rehired more than 350 previously laid off engineers. According to Ford, the AI driven automated systems did not perform at the rate that the company believe they would. Cloud compute prices have soared, making the use of LLM models not as price friendly as it once was. Nvidia reports that the compute price for AI models often exceeds the salary of the employee replaced.
